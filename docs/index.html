How it works

  1. 01

    Write one line

    Share a real line that sounds like you.

  2. 02

    Add a few clues

    Signals like how you chat, your rhythm, and what you want to talk about.

  3. 03

    Discover people you can get along with

    See people through their one line and clues, and notice who feels like a natural fit.

  4. 04

    Send a live invite

    Invite someone to chat while both of you are open in the moment.

  5. 05

    Chat when both sides are interested

    Conversations begin only when the interest goes both ways.
