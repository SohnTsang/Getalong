Common questions

Sign in
Getalong signs you in with Apple, Google, or X. Use the same provider you started with. If it still won't connect, email us the provider you use and we'll look into it.
Report or block someone
You can block any user and report content from inside the app — on a card or inside a chat. Blocking takes effect immediately. If you're in danger, contact your local authorities first.
Delete account or data
Open Profile → Delete account to remove your account and associated data, or email support@getalong.app and we'll handle it for you.
TestFlight feedback
Testing an early build? We'd love your notes. Reply in TestFlight or email us what you saw, the device you're on, and what you expected to happen.
